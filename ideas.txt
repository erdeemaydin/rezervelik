# Future Ideas for Restaurant Management SaaS

## 2. CRM & Guest Loyalty Profiles
Build a rich CRM (Customer Relationship Management) where the system remembers guests. 
- **Features:** Track visit frequency, total money spent, dietary restrictions, favorite tables, and VIP status. 
- **Use Case:** When "John Doe" calls, the staff instantly sees it's his 5th visit and that he is highly allergic to peanuts.

## 3. Yield Management & Premium Seating (Revenue Generator)
Allow restaurants to monetize their best tables (e.g., "Window Seat with Ocean View"). 
- **Features:** Customers can pay a non-refundable "premium seating fee" to guarantee that specific table, or the system dynamically requires higher deposits for Friday night prime-time slots (Dynamic Pricing).

## 4. Pre-Ordering & Upselling
During the online booking flow, allow guests to pre-order items.
- **Features:** "Would you like a bottle of Champagne waiting at the table for your anniversary?" This increases the restaurant's average check size before the guest even walks in the door.

## 5. Automated "No-Show" Prevention (SMS/WhatsApp)
No-shows are the biggest problem for restaurants. 
- **Features:** Integrate Twilio or a local Turkish SMS gateway. Automatically send a reminder 24 hours before the reservation with a link: `[Confirm] or [Cancel]`. If they don't click confirm, the system flags the reservation in red for the host to call them manually.

## 6. POS (Point of Sale) Integration
While much harder to build, this is the "holy grail".
- **Features:** Sync with systems like Toast, Simphony, or local Turkish POS systems. The moment the waiter prints the bill on the POS, the reservation system automatically turns the table status to "Paying", letting the host know the table is about to free up.

## 8. Automated AI Review Management
Since you already have a Reviews page placeholder, you can wire up an LLM (like Claude or GPT-4). When a 3-star review comes in on Google Maps, the AI automatically drafts a polite, customized apology and offers a contact email, saving the manager hours of typing.
